# German texts for stock V1.2.0, for build_link_image.py --texts.
#
# Each line replaces one text: SLOT WAS FONT WIDTH HEIGHT TEXT (see texts/es.txt for what
# each field is and what the builder refuses).
#
# NOT REVIEWED BY A NATIVE SPEAKER AND NOT SEEN ON A UNIT. The terms are the usual ones for
# an instrument, and the builder and stock's own text routine (in emulation) say they are
# drawn whole. Whether they read well is for someone who speaks the language to say:
# corrections are welcome.
#

# The oscilloscope's menu (box of 76 x 36, 18 px font: two lines). Stock's first seven entries
# are single words wider than the box, which the routine cuts where they stop fitting, in the
# middle of a syllable. The routine only ends a line at a space, so each of these is written
# with a hyphen and a space where German would divide the word: the text is wider than the box
# and the space ends the first line, which leaves the hyphen at its end. The singular
# "Einstellung" is used because the plural is wider than the box.

0x0804C8C8 0x080BC39F 18 76 36 Cursor- Messung
0x0804C8CC 0x080BC3AE 18 76 36 Parameter- messung
0x0804C8D0 0x080BC793 18 76 36 CH1- Einstellung

0x0804C8D8 0x080BC7D1 18 76 36 Trigger- Einstellung
0x0804C8DC 0x080BC3BF 18 76 36 Nach- leuchten
