# Russian texts for stock V1.2.0, for build_link_image.py --texts.
#
# Each line replaces one text: SLOT WAS FONT WIDTH HEIGHT TEXT (see texts/es.txt for what
# each field is and what the builder refuses).
#
# NOT REVIEWED BY A NATIVE SPEAKER AND NOT SEEN ON A UNIT. The terms are the usual ones for
# an instrument, and the builder and stock's own text routine (in emulation) say they are
# drawn whole. Whether they read well is for someone who speaks the language to say:
# corrections are welcome.
#
# First, what stock does not draw whole: the multimeter's titles that do not fit their box of
# 216 x 29 (28 px font), and the generator's third label, whose second line falls off the
# screen (box of 54 x 22, 18 px font).
# The fonts lack fifteen Cyrillic letters, most of them capitals (among them the capitals of
# г, ж, й, у, ф, х, ш, щ, ы, ь, э, ю, and the small ъ and э): a text that needs one is refused.

0x0804C4BC 0x080BDEC4 28 216 29 Пост. ток (мА)

# The oscilloscope's menu (box of 76 x 36, 18 px font: two lines). Stock's first, third,
# seventh and eighth entries have a word wider than the box, which the routine cuts where it
# stops fitting; of the third the end goes to a third line, on top of the next entry. The seventh is
# also the wrong word: stock has the one for an afterword where the entry is the persistence
# of the trace. It is written with a hyphen and a space where Russian would divide the word:
# the routine only ends a line at a space, and the text being wider than the box, that space
# ends the first line and leaves the hyphen at its end.
0x0804C844 0x080BD2EB 18 76 36 Режим дисплея
0x0804C84C 0x080BDC8A 18 76 36 Измерение параметров
0x0804C85C 0x080BDC62 18 76 36 После- свечение
0x0804C860 0x080BD30D 18 76 36 Математика
