# Spanish texts for stock V1.2.0, for build_link_image.py --texts.
#
# Each line replaces one text: SLOT WAS FONT WIDTH HEIGHT TEXT
#   SLOT    address of the pointer in stock's table (hex)
#   WAS     the pointer stock has there (hex): the builder refuses anything else
#   FONT    height in px of the font the text is drawn in
#   WIDTH   width and height in px of the box it is drawn in. Stock breaks a text that is too
#   HEIGHT  wide into lines at its spaces, and what does not fit the height is lost or lands
#           on something else; the builder refuses a text that stock would not draw whole
#   TEXT    the new text, to the end of the line
#
# The multimeter's title: table at 0x0804C43C, twelve functions for each of eight languages,
# drawn in the 28 px font in a box of 216 x 29 (0x08015C08). Spanish is the fifth row. Stock's
# Spanish names for the voltages and the currents are 22 to 29 characters long and lose the
# word that says whether it is DC or AC; these fit. CC and CA are the usual Spanish
# abbreviations, and the unit says which of the two current ranges it is.
0x0804C500 0x080BBF63 28 216 29 Tensión continua
0x0804C504 0x080BBED1 28 216 29 Tensión alterna
0x0804C51C 0x080BBF99 28 216 29 Corriente CC (mA)
0x0804C520 0x080BBF05 28 216 29 Corriente CA (mA)
0x0804C524 0x080BBF81 28 216 29 Corriente CC (A)
0x0804C528 0x080BBEEE 28 216 29 Corriente CA (A)

# The generator's three settings: table at 0x0804C5BC, three labels for each language, drawn
# in the 18 px font in a box of 54 x 22 (0x08015988), 32 px apart. Stock's Spanish for the
# third is one pixel too wide: its last letter goes to a second line, which for the last row
# is the bottom edge of the screen. The first two take two lines as well, and theirs are on
# the screen, but cut where the box ends and not at a syllable ("Frecuen / cia", "Ciclo de /
# trabajo" on the unit); these are the usual abbreviations and fit in one. On the unit since
# 2026-11-27.
0x0804C5EC 0x080BBE46 18 54 22 Frec.
0x0804C5F0 0x080BCA28 18 54 22 Ciclo
0x0804C5F4 0x080BC085 18 54 22 Amplit.

# The generator's waveforms: table at 0x0804C61C, thirteen names for each language, drawn in
# the 18 px font in a box of 100 x 36 (0x0801579C), so two lines fit. These replace names
# that are not what the waveform is called in Spanish: the sawtooth (stock has "tooth wave"),
# the two staircases ("step" translated as a pace), the two exponentials (the rising one is
# a word-for-word "index up") and the sinc pulse ("sink").
0x0804C6F4 0x080BC29F 18 100 36 Diente de sierra
0x0804C700 0x080BCA5F 18 100 36 Escalera
0x0804C704 0x080BCA6C 18 100 36 Escalera inversa
0x0804C708 0x080BBD34 18 100 36 Subida exponencial
0x0804C70C 0x080BC6F2 18 100 36 Bajada exponencial
0x0804C718 0x080BC6C9 18 100 36 Pulso sinc

# The oscilloscope's menu: table at 0x0804C804, eight entries for each language, six drawn
# at a time by 0x08012DE0 in the 18 px font in a box of 76 x 36, so two lines of 76 px.
# Stock's "Configuración" is 88 px wide: the routine breaks it after "Configuraci", and of
# the third entry the end falls on a third line that is not drawn ("Configuraci / ón de
# dispa"). The other five entries fit and stay.
0x0804C890 0x080BB933 18 76 36 Ajustes de CH1
0x0804C894 0x080BB983 18 76 36 Ajustes de CH2
0x0804C898 0x080BCA39 18 76 36 Ajustes de disparo
