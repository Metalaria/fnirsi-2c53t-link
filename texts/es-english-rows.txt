# Spanish for the texts stock V1.2.0 has in Chinese and English only, for build_link_image.py --texts.
#
# Each line replaces one text: SLOT WAS FONT WIDTH HEIGHT TEXT (see texts/es.txt for what
# each field is and what the builder refuses).
#
# READ THIS FIRST. These tables have two rows, Chinese and English, and every language but
# Chinese is shown the English one. Replacing a text here therefore changes it for English,
# Russian, Japanese, Portuguese, German and Korean as well: an instrument built with this
# file says "Canal:" whatever language it is set to, except Chinese. Use it for a unit that
# is going to stay in Spanish, together with texts/es.txt.

# A channel's settings (0x0801333x): labels from the table at 0x08014198, drawn in the 18 px
# font in a box of 100 x 32, left-aligned. "FFT:" stays.
0x080141AC 0x080BBA5A 18 100 32 Canal:
0x080141B0 0x080BBA2A 18 100 32 Sonda:
0x080141B4 0x080BBA3D 18 100 32 Acoplamiento:

# The trigger's settings (0x0801369x): labels from 0x0804C7CC in the same box; the mode from
# 0x0804C7E4 in a box of 66 x 20, and again in the bar at the bottom of the screen
# (0x0801E784), which measures it. "Normal" and "Auto" stay. Spanish would say "Único" for
# a single shot, and the fonts have no u with an acute accent.
0x0804C7D8 0x080BBA31 18 100 32 Modo:
0x0804C7DC 0x080BBA37 18 100 32 Flanco:
0x0804C7E0 0x080BBA5A 18 100 32 Canal:
0x0804C7F8 0x080BC184 18 66 20 Simple

# The multimeter's three boxes (0x0801CAE6): labels from 0x0804C3D8, 20 px font, boxes of
# 100 x 20. "MAX" and "MIN" stay.
0x0804C3E8 0x080BBC4C 20 100 20 MEDIA

# The oscilloscope's measurements (0x0801BB8A): labels from 0x0804BF28, printed in front of
# the value and its unit ("%s%.2f%s" or "%s%.1f%s") and drawn together, in the 15 px font,
# in a box of 100 x 15. What the builder can check is the label alone, so the width given
# here is that of stock's widest label, "Duty+:", 36 px. Each of these is as wide as the
# label it replaces or narrower, but "Med:", 26 px for the 24 of "Avg:"; the six voltages
# share their formats and units (mV, V), and stock's "Vmin:" is 31 px.
# "Vp-p:" and "Amp:" stay.

0x0804BF2C 0x080BBA4C 15 36 15 Per:
0x0804BF30 0x080BB9F8 15 36 15 Ciclo+:

0x0804BF3C 0x080BB9FF 15 36 15 Anc-:
